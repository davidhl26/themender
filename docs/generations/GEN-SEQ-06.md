**Style prompt (EN)** : Desaturated grey-blue night. The open laptop screen is the only key light — cold blue-white on the girl's face, shifting faintly as unseen pages scroll — with the sodium orange of the street lamp through the window as a thin rim on her hair, deep blue-black shadow everywhere else.
