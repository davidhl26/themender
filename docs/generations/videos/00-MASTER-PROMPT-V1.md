# MASTER PROMPT — VIDÉO 1  ·  Seedance 2.5  ·  le gabarit dont les 45 autres découlent

> **Ce qu'il fait de nouveau.** Les plans 1.2, 1.3 et 1.4 du script — jusqu'ici trois générations
> et deux raccords — deviennent **une seule prise de 24 s**. Les deux raccords disparaissent : la
> continuité n'est plus demandée, elle est mécanique.
>
> **Ce qu'il emprunte au prompt de référence.** La liste d'étapes datées, et surtout **la
> TRANSITION écrite entre chacune** : chaque étape sort sur un mouvement, la suivante entre sur ce
> même mouvement. Un vecteur se transmet mieux qu'une coordonnée. Les répliques sont **dans**
> l'étape où elles tombent, jamais dans une section à part qui peut dériver.
>
> **Ce qu'il garde de moi.** La géométrie canonique de la pièce, les exclusions par référence
> (dont l'audio), et la dernière frame chiffrée pour le plan suivant.

MATERIALS — @image1 IS THE FIRST FRAME OF THIS GENERATION. @sambefore is SAM. @maeva is MAEVE. @kitchen is THE KITCHEN. No video is attached: this is the head of the chain and it sets the light, the grain and the skin rendering every later shot will be matched to.
ROLES — From the first-frame image take the opening composition and the exact position, pose and prop state of everything in it; take nothing else from it, no border, no backdrop, no empty-room staging, no reference layout. From SAM, MAEVE and the kitchen reference take identity, face, build, wardrobe, architecture and materials exactly; take none of their pose, framing, lighting or staging.

STYLE — Kodak Vision3 500T 5219 tungsten colour negative, printed flat: cool naturalistic colour, shadows slightly blue-green and holding texture, skin understated and never rosy, highlights restrained and never blown, a faint halation ring around the window, moderate fine grain. Not glossy, not digital. EDITING GRAMMAR: THERE IS NO EDITING — one single unbroken take, 24 seconds, real time, no cut, no dissolve, no speed ramp, no slow motion. The only change of framing is the one the camera performs on its own feet in stage 4. Anamorphic 40 mm f/2.8 throughout: the frame tightens because the camera walks, never because it zooms.

AUDIO DESIGN — SFX only, and the sound thins as the shot goes. NO MUSIC ANYWHERE: no score, no drone, no ambient pad, at any point. First the room: the low sizzle and thin crackle of burnt bread, the breath of the gas dying, a small radio two metres away playing something too quiet to identify, one gull far off outside. Then the chair: the short dry scrape of its legs on tile as his weight leaves it, a small creak of wood. Then three steps in wool socks, almost silent, one soft drag on the third. Then the rustle of wool as the embrace closes. Then a TRUE SILENCE holding the last four seconds, with only two sets of breathing that DO NOT fall into step, the pan ticking as it cools, and two low lines inside it. Clean dialogue, close, unprocessed.

CHARACTERS, and only what the reference sheets CANNOT carry — SAM: he narrates his own failures while they happen and laughs at himself; he touches people as he passes them; nothing guarded in the shoulders. HIS HANDS ARE CLEAN, NO INK ANYWHERE. MAEVE: dry, fast, affectionate, entirely unsentimental — her tenderness arrives as instruction, never as comfort. SHE WEARS NO SCARF OF ANY KIND: the red scarf of her reference sheet does not exist yet in the film. Face, build and wardrobe come from the references and are not restated here. NEITHER OF THEM PERFORMS AN EMOTION: the feeling is legible only through the physical events written below, and through nothing else.

THE ROOM, only what a single reference image cannot fix — THE GAS RANGE WITH THE SINGLE WINDOW DIRECTLY ABOVE IT is the far EAST wall, the sill just above the back burners, no cabinet over the range: it is the deepest landmark and the ONLY source of light. The sink, the cluttered counter and its small radio run along that same wall to its left; the open shelves of cream and blue crockery hold the north wall; THE DOORWAY TO THE DARK HALLWAY is in the south wall and stays empty for the whole take. The worn wooden table and its four mismatched chairs stand free in the middle of the room, roughly 3.6 m by 3.0 m. ON THE TABLE, exactly five things and never anything else: an open jar of orange marmalade with a teaspoon standing in it at the centre, a pat of butter on a cream-and-blue dish, one empty bowl at the near place, his steaming cup beside it, a second steaming cup at the far place with its chair EMPTY AND PUSHED IN. NO bread, NO toast, NO plate on the table — the only toast in existence is the two blackened slices in the cast-iron pan on the front-left burner, smoking very thinly under the window.

SCREEN DIRECTION, and it is not negotiable — the camera stays on the doorway side of the room for the whole take. SAM holds SCREEN-LEFT in every instant of this generation, MAEVE holds SCREEN-RIGHT, THE GAS RANGE AND ITS WINDOW are behind her, deepest in frame. This never inverts, not once.
LIGHT — ONE SOURCE ONLY: the single window directly above the gas range, straight behind them, throwing warm gold morning light toward the lens. High soft key, warm ambers, open shadows, NOTHING underexposed, no grey, no cold cast. One hard bar of it lies diagonally across the table with dust turning inside it. No lamp, no practical, no fill, no bounce card, no second source. The blue gas flame is the only cool note and it dies in the first stage.

STAGE 1 (0:00-0:01,3) — Wide across the room from the west side, eye level 145 cm, operated and breathing, carried rather than nailed down. MAEVE stands at the range screen-right at x=68%, half-silhouetted in the window's gold, her back three-quarter to camera. SAM is SEATED at the table screen-left at x=30%, three-quarter to camera, turned toward her, a spoon in his hand. Her right hand reaches down to the front-left knob and TURNS THE GAS OFF — the blue flame shrinks and dies, the sizzle sinking to a whisper. TRANSITION: the dying of the flame turns her; she pivots on the spot toward the room.
STAGE 2 (0:01,3-0:06) — Enters on that pivot: SHE COMES ROUND, her back settling against the range rail and both hands coming to rest on its edge either side of her hips, and she finds his eyes across the room. Half a confession and half a laugh, MAEVE: "I think I burned the toast." A beat of silence, the pan crackling. Gentle, deadpan, no edge, SAM from the table without moving: "Why don't you use the toaster?" TRANSITION: the question lands and her chin lifts a centimetre, the amusement starting in the eyes before the mouth.
STAGE 3 (0:06-0:11) — Enters on that lifted chin. Relaxed, matter-of-fact, the amusement rising on the last word, a real pause inside the line, MAEVE: "It's broken." — pause — "We're saving money." A WARM SMILING SILENCE, the two of them looking at each other across the kitchen, both wearing small growing smiles, the steam of the two cups crossing the bar of light between them. He sets the spoon down beside his bowl without a sound and FLATTENS BOTH PALMS ON THE TABLE EDGE, eyes never leaving her. TRANSITION: the weight goes into those two flat palms — the whole rise begins there.
STAGE 4 (0:11-0:16) — Enters on the weight in his palms: HE RISES, AND THE WHOLE RISE IS SEEN. The fingers flatten and go a shade paler against the wood, the tendons standing on the backs of his hands, his shoulders coming forward over the table, his chin dropping, and then he is up. Nothing is skipped and nothing happens off-screen. HE CROSSES, three unhurried steps on the tile, his weight rolling heel to ball, the wool socks almost silent, one soft drag on the third step — AND THE CAMERA GOES WITH HIM, drifting right and forward at his pace, a beat behind him, an operator walking, never a dolly on rails, arriving by 0:16 at a tighter two-shot of the two of them chest-up and half-silhouetted against the window, where it settles and breathes for the rest of the take. TRANSITION: he stops face to face with her and the camera stops with him, both of them still moving very slightly.
STAGE 5 (0:16-0:20) — Enters on that arrival, close. A HELD BEAT, FACE TO FACE: her chin tips up two centimetres to keep his eyes, his head tilts one degree, she breathes in and it lifts her shoulders a centimetre and lets them down. Then HIS ARMS GO AROUND HER AND THE MOVEMENT IS SEEN WHOLE — the right arm first and low, sliding around the small of her back, the left following a beat later and higher, his hands coming flat with the fingers spread and the wool of her sweater gathering under his palms. Her two hands come flat on his chest, one slightly higher than the other. Their foreheads settle together. TRANSITION: everything stops except the breathing.
STAGE 6 (0:20-0:24) — Enters on total stillness, foreheads resting against each other, both pairs of eyes closed. NOBODY MOVES except their breathing, and THE TWO BREATHS DO NOT FALL INTO STEP — his slower and deeper, hers shallower and quicker, crossing each other. Whispered, without opening her eyes and without moving her head, her lips the only thing in her face that moves, MAEVE: "Love you." A breath; HIS CHEST LIFTS ONCE UNDER HER TWO HANDS and her hands ride it up and down. Then LOWER than she was, certain, just as quietly, SAM: "Love you more." Held to the end with no further movement beyond their living micro-life.
THE SPOKEN LINES OF THIS GENERATION ARE EXACTLY THESE FOUR AND NO OTHERS: "I think I burned the toast." / "Why don't you use the toaster?" / "It's broken." "We're saving money." / "Love you." / "Love you more." — no line is repeated, no line is added, nobody else speaks, and there is no voice-over. Between 0:11 and 0:20 NOBODY SPEAKS AT ALL: no word, no murmur, no breath shaped like speech.

ENDING FRAME, the exact state this generation hands over — The two of them chest-up and half-silhouetted in the window's gold, forehead resting against forehead, both pairs of eyes closed; her two hands flat on his chest one slightly higher than the other; his arms wrapped around her waist with the wool of her sweater gathered under his palms; SAM centre-left at x=44% filling 72% of frame height, MAEVE centre-right at x=58% filling 70%; one last thin thread of smoke rising from the cooling pan beside them under the window; the laid table untouched behind them at the lower right frame edge.
AVOID — a kiss, kissing, mouths touching, the embrace played long or theatrical, tears, crying, sadness, any spoken line other than the five written above, a toaster, any toaster visible anywhere, a flame under the pan after stage 1, the toast leaving the pan, toast or bread or a plate on the table, food being served, a knife or utensil in anyone's hands, a third person in the kitchen, a silhouette in the doorway, a red scarf, a scarf of any kind on the woman, any red anywhere, a cut of any kind, a dissolve, a zoom, a crash zoom, a whip pan, slow motion, a speed ramp, the camera pushing in on its own, music of any kind, on-screen text, subtitles, captions, either of them looking at camera, visible camera rigs.
