## GÉNÉRATION 7A — plans 7.1–7.4 « Elle cherche » *(12 s — quatre plans, hard cuts, 3 s → 2,5 s)*

**Elements** : @Nora + @BackGallery + @NoraBedroom + @Kitchen · **Settings** : Genre Noir · Camera Fine Film · Lens Anamorphic 50mm f/2 · 21:9 · 1080p · 12s · sound on

**Style prompt (EN)** : Days passing in fragments — each shot its own day and light: flat white morning, colourless noon, blue night, grey dawn; desaturated grey-blues throughout, motivated sources only, deep blacks, camera locked off in every shot, no handheld shake. Gently underexposed, sparse motivated sources, deep blacks. Naturalistic, lived-in; real time inside each shot — the acceleration lives only in the cutting. Hyper-realistic live-action footage, indistinguishable from film shot on a real set with real actors: true skin texture with visible pores and fine hairs, real fabric weave, seams and wrinkles that crease with movement, natural motion blur, constant micro-life in every held position (breathing, blinking, weight shifts of a few millimetres), only motivated practical light, subtle film grain, true-to-life colour. NOT CGI, NOT animation, no uncanny smoothness, no plastic skin, no beauty retouching, no video-game look.

**SCENE** Days pass in four short shots, each shorter than the last. A girl still looking for signs — the street, the mailbox, the screen — and the looking wearing out. No dialogue. Every second is choreographed below; nothing beyond it may be invented. **Each HARD CUT is a scripted time jump: a different day, a different light — nothing needs to match across the cuts except her.**

**PERFORMANCE PACING** Unhurried, naturalistic, lived-in performance throughout. Real-time pacing inside each shot: every action takes the time it takes in real life, nothing is compressed or hurried. Held looks are truly held. Documentary naturalism, warm and imperfect, never theatrical.

**CONTINUITY LOCK** Every prop named in the layout appears in EVERY shot where its surface is visible, in the same position and state — nothing appears, disappears, multiplies or moves unless the choreography says so. Each character's posture is stated at the top of every shot and never changes off-screen. A character holds exactly the object the choreography puts in their hand — no other object may materialise. Across the scripted time jumps only @Nora persists — same face, same wardrobe logic.

**PROP LAYOUT — FIXED** Shot 1: the gallery rail, bare; the washing line, empty. Shot 2: the weathered grey-green sheet-metal mailbox on its porch post, door hanging open, EMPTY inside, no name, no number, no lettering. Shot 3: the desk of @NoraBedroom, the laptop OPEN this once, screen a soft blue blur (all text [POST]), the pile of school books unmoved. Shot 4: the kitchen table of @Kitchen, one bowl, one spoon, one glass of water — one place laid, nothing else; the other chairs empty and pushed in; **no marmalade, no coffee cups, no butter dish — the breakfast table of sequence 1 no longer exists.**

**POSTURE LOCK** Shot 1: @Nora STANDING at the rail. Shot 2: @Nora STANDING at the mailbox. Shot 3: @Nora SEATED at the desk. Shot 4: @Nora SEATED at the kitchen table. She never walks within a shot.

**FRAME MAP & CHOREOGRAPHY — SECOND BY SECOND**

**SHOT 1 [0.0-3.0s] — Medium shot on the gallery, eye level, static, locked off. Flat white MORNING.**
[0.0s] Starting positions: @Nora STANDING at the gallery rail, screen-left at x=35%, chest-up, parka over hoodie, hood down, both hands on the rail. Her eyes are ALIVE — scanning.
[0.0-3.0s] Her eyes sweep the street below, left to right, unhurried; then lift to the rooftops; then drop to the yards. Searching, still believing. Nothing answers. Held to the cut mid-search.

**HARD CUT — ANOTHER DAY**

**SHOT 2 [3.0-6.0s] — Close shot on the mailbox, eye level, static, locked off. Colourless NOON.**
[3.0s] Starting positions: the open mailbox centred at x=50%; @Nora beside it screen-right at x=70%, in profile, parka, hood down.
[3.0-4.4s] Her right hand is INSIDE the mailbox — and stays there a full second, feeling the emptiness, hoping the hand is wrong.
[4.4-5.2s] The hand comes out, empty. She looks up the street one way — then the other. Nothing.
[5.2-6.0s] She pushes the little door shut with two fingers, slowly. It clicks. Held.

**HARD CUT — NIGHT**

**SHOT 3 [6.0-9.0s] — Medium close over her shoulder at the desk, static, locked off. Blue NIGHT.**
[6.0s] Starting positions: @Nora SEATED at the desk of her room, three-quarter back to camera at x=45%, hoodie, the open laptop's blue glow on her face's edge; the screen a soft blur (all text [POST]).
[6.0-7.6s] She reads — motionless — then her right index finger presses one key, once: refresh. The glow flickers faintly.
[7.6-9.0s] Nothing changes on the blur. Her shoulders sink one centimetre. Held on her stillness.

**HARD CUT — GREY DAWN**

**SHOT 4 [9.0-11.5s] — Medium shot across the kitchen table, eye level, static, locked off. Grey DAWN, no gold anywhere.**
[9.0s] Starting positions: @Nora SEATED alone at the kitchen table at x=45%, hoodie, one bowl before her, spoon in her right hand; the other chairs empty and pushed in; the window a flat grey rectangle — the bar of golden light of sequence 1 does not exist.
[9.0-10.3s] She eats — two slow mechanical spoonfuls, eyes down, tasting nothing.
[10.3-11.5s] Mid-spoonful her eyes slide to the window — ONE LAST TIME — hold half a second… and come back down to the bowl. That was the last look. Held to the end.

**SUBJECT LOCK, NORA** The exact girl of the reference @Nora — fifteen, thin, long dark brown wavy unwashed hair, chapped lips, grey-green eyes with shadowed hollows; charcoal hoodie, dark navy parka (shots 1-2), dark indigo jeans, scuffed off-white sneakers. Arc across the four shots: the eyes do all the acting — alive and scanning in shot 1, hopeful in shot 2, flat in shot 3, down in shot 4. No tears anywhere. She never looks at the lens.

**CROSS-FRAME RULES** The same girl in all four shots, exact face of her reference, same wardrobe logic. Each shot is its own location and its own day — the cuts are scripted time jumps; only she persists. Locations are exactly their reference Elements. **No other person, no silhouette, no shadow of anyone, in any shot.** No red anywhere. NO INVENTED ACTION: If a body part is not described as moving, it does not move. No gesture, step, head turn, prop interaction or expression change beyond the choreography written above; between described movements every body holds its last described position naturally (breathing, blinking, micro-sway only). PERFORMANCE PACING and CONTINUITY LOCK apply as written above.

**LOCATION** @BackGallery (shot 1 — the rail's paint peeling in curled flakes and rubbed to bare grey wood where hands rest, boards split at the ends with rusted nail heads standing proud, the washing line slack and frayed, its fibres fuzzed along the sag) · the porch mailbox one-off (shot 2 — rust blooming through the grey-green paint along the folded seams and around the hinge, the door on a loosened pin, the post's wood checked and grey) · @NoraBedroom (shot 3 — the desk's varnish worn through at the front edge, old water rings, the dog-eared books unmoved, the sagging mattress deep in shadow) · @Kitchen, grey version (shot 4 — the same room gone cold: the cast-iron pan cold on its burner under a window that is only a flat grey rectangle, the tea towel limp over the oven rail, the table's varnish dull and its old knife-marks flat in the shadowless light, the crockery dim on the shelves).

**LIGHT** One light per day, each physically motivated and gently underexposed: flat white morning — a high overcast sky as one huge soft source, shadowless, the boards pale and cold (1); colourless noon — dull vertical daylight that flattens the mailbox's metal and kills every highlight (2); night — the laptop's blue glow the only key, dying before it reaches the walls (3); grey dawn — skylight before any sun, the kitchen window a flat luminous rectangle that casts nothing (4). Sparse motivated sources, deep blacks. Never the gold of sequence 1.

**DIALOGUE** None. Not a word in the whole sequence.

**LAST FRAME** Her at the kitchen table, alone, eyes back down on the bowl, spoon mid-air, the window a flat grey rectangle behind her, the empty chairs pushed in.

**AUDIO** One thin sound per shot, no more: gulls far off and a low wind working the gallery boards (1); the mailbox door's click — one dry snap of thin sheet metal on the empty street (2); one key press and the room's silence closing back over it (3); the spoon against the bowl, twice, small in the empty kitchen (4). Under each, only the faint tone of the living world — distant town, air in the room — barely there, never a second event. Between them, the cuts are dry. No music — **le thème n'existe pas encore ; le silence EST la séquence.**

**NEGATIVE PROMPT** visible camera rigs, cartoonish colors, blurred focus on her, on-screen text, readable screen content, subjects looking at camera, slow motion, time-lapse, speed ramp, fast motion, morphing objects, extra people in frame, any second person, any silhouette or human shadow, extra gestures, improvised actions, wandering hands, repositioned props, appearing or disappearing objects, mail in the mailbox, letters, a phone, tears, crying, rushed movements inside a shot, theatrical acting, overacting, any red anywhere, warm golden light, marmalade or coffee or butter on the table, modern branding, readable signage, uncanny smoothness, plastic skin, beauty filter, CGI look, video-game lighting, generated music.
