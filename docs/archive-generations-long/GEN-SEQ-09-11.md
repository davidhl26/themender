### BLOC 9-A — La vitrine, la table, la quatrième tasse *(9 s · plans 10.1 · 10.2 · 10.3+10.4)*
*Note : 10.3 et 10.4 sont générés comme UN segment continu — le geste de la quatrième tasse est
un seul geste, la coupe du montage reste possible sur l'immobilisation de la main.*

```
Elements @Sam + @Nora + @Milo + @Mei + @Restaurant. 21:9, 1080p, 9s, sound on.
Genre Noir · Style Manual · Camera Fine Film · Lens Anamorphic, 50mm, f/2.
Style prompt: amber paper lanterns and brass as the only warm sources, dark green tablecloths,
the fish tank glowing green at frame edge, the shop sign reduced to a soft amber halo on the
fogged pane, the street outside cold and blue, deep blacks, camera locked off, no handheld shake.

SCENE
A family dinner at a table set for four, one chair empty. Three shots along one evening,
hard cuts; the third shot is one continuous insert.

FRAME MAP
[0-2.5s] Exterior. The fogged restaurant window fills the frame, centred; behind the glass,
three blurred silhouettes at a table set for four, centre, x=50%, mid-ground; a sodium street
lamp at the far left edge, x=8%; the shop sign only a soft amber halo, upper right, x=85%.
[2.5-5s] Interior. The back of the empty fourth chair large and soft in the near foreground,
lower centre, x=50%, holding the bottom of the frame. Beyond it, second plane: @Sam screen-left,
x=30%, filling 45% of frame height; @Milo small beside him, x=48%; @Nora screen-right, x=70%.
[5-9s] Insert, high angle at 45 degrees over the tabletop. Green cloth filling the frame. Cups
set down at x=30%, x=45%, x=62%; the fourth enters from screen-right toward the empty place at
x=80%. The hands of @Mei work from the right edge. Her face never in frame.

SUBJECT LOCK, @SAM
Exact face, mid-length unkempt greying beard, olive canvas work jacket and ink-stained
fingertips of the reference. Heavy, silent, shoulders slumped, eyes down for the full runtime.

SUBJECT LOCK, @NORA
Exact face of the reference — heavy lids, shadowed hollows, chapped lips. Oversized charcoal
hoodie. Her plate stays full and untouched. She does not speak and does not look up.

SUBJECT LOCK, @MILO
Exact face of the reference, mustard-yellow sleeve. The only one at the table moving happily.

SUBJECT LOCK, @MEI (hands only in this block)
Sturdy kitchen-worn hands, a thin jade bangle on the left wrist, dark green apron edge in
frame. Her face is never in frame. The gesture is habitual, fluid, unthinking — then it stops.

CROSS-FRAME RULES
@Restaurant is the same room in all three shots: the window of shot one is the window of the
room in shot two, same half-down roller blind, same amber lanterns, same green cloths, same
fish tank. The three silhouettes behind the glass in shot one are @Sam, @Milo and @Nora of
shot two, in the same seats. The tabletop of shot three is the family table of shot two, same
green cloth, same lantern overhead. The fourth chair is never taken away. Wardrobe identical
throughout. Nobody looks at the lens.

LOCATION
@Restaurant — a small old family-run Chinese restaurant in a New England port town at night:
six formica tables with dark green cloths, a lacquered counter, a green-lit fish tank, a
beaded curtain to the kitchen, paper lanterns in amber and brass, a window onto a wet street
with the roller blind half down.

LIGHT
Interior: low amber lanterns over the tables, the counter warm, the fish tank throwing green
at the edge. Exterior: cold white room light diffused through the fogged glass, one sodium
lamp on the street, the sign never a light source — only a soft amber halo on the wet pane.

MOVEMENT
[0-2.5s] Static from across the wet street. Rain runs down the fogged glass. Through the
condensation, the three silhouettes sit at the table set for four — soft, indistinct, no faces
readable. Nobody inside moves toward the window; nobody looks out.
HARD CUT
[2.5-5s] Static over the back of the empty fourth chair. @Sam heavy and silent, @Milo small
beside him, @Nora across with her plate untouched. Other diners far behind, seen only from
behind or fully out of focus. The fourth chair has not been taken away.
HARD CUT
[5-9s] One continuous insert. The hands of @Mei set down white tea cups on the green cloth —
one. Two. Three. Then a fourth cup enters the frame in her hand, moving toward the empty
place. The hand stops mid-air, half a beat of total stillness. She takes the fourth cup back
up and carries it out of frame. The three remaining cups stay exactly where they were. A very
slight camera pull-back during the removal, nothing more. Mute gesture — no face, no comment.

DIALOGUE
None.

CAMERA
[0-2.5s] Wide shot, eye level, static, from across the street.
[2.5-5s] Wide shot, eye level, static, shallow focus past the chair back.
[5-9s] Insert, high angle at 45 degrees, static, then a very slight pull-back.

LAST FRAME
The tabletop from above: three white cups on the dark green cloth, the fourth place empty,
the hand of @Mei leaving the frame on the right.

AUDIO
Rain on glass and pavement, a foghorn far off; then the dining room — the kitchen off-screen,
low conversations, the fish tank pump; four small porcelain knocks, then a single fifth knock,
duller, felt as an absence, as the fourth cup is taken back. No music.

NEGATIVE PROMPT
visible camera rigs, cartoonish colors, blurred focus, on-screen text, subject looking at
camera, slow motion, morphing objects, modern branding, readable signage, legible lettering
on the window, any red in frame, red lanterns, red signage, red neon, red tablecloths, red
menus, red chopsticks, red packaging, red porcelain, @Mei's face in frame, the fourth chair
removed, a fourth cup left on the table at the end, extra hands entering frame
```

### BLOC 9-C — Al-Maktoub, Moyiren, le geste *(12,5 s · plans 10.9 · 10.10 · 10.11)*

Two accounts, one after the other — the man who went back to the boats, the girl who spoke

[7-9s] @Mei, quick, already halfway into the next thought: "He stopped a girl for directions."
[9.8-14s] @Mei, rapid, fragmented, cutting herself off: "This girl hadn't talked since the

CAMERA
[0-3s] Insert, eye level across the table, static.
[3-6.5s] Medium close-up, eye level, static.
[6.5-9.5s] Close-up, high angle on the hands, very slight push-in.
[9.5-14.5s] Close-up, slightly low angle (she is standing), static.
